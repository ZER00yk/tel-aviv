# Night Shift

Real private 4–12 player rooms with server-owned private roles, movement/proximity checks, cooperative tasks, hostile elimination/cooldowns, timed sabotage/repair, reporting, emergency meetings, voting/ejection, and team win conditions. Alive/dead discussion is partitioned by the server.

## Controls

Create/join room, ready all players, then host starts. WASD/arrows or touch moves. Crew complete nearby assigned tasks; hostiles eliminate near crew or sabotage. Repair north beacon; call meetings at center, report bodies, discuss and vote.

## State and trust

Lobby state and per-user redacted views are realtime-service-owned. No hidden role information is included for other players.

## First-pass limits

Initial roles are Crew/Hostile; custom/neutral roles, richer station rooms/objective interactions, persistent match restoration after process restart, and spectating remain follow-up.
