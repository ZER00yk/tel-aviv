# Empire Exchange

Businesses, hiring, upgrades, pricing/demand, research, reputation, and server-time offline operation. Four industries progress from a kiosk to a dream studio.

## Controls

Open a kiosk first, serve customers for initial revenue, then hire workers to automate. Tune price against hourly demand; research twice to unlock the studio.

## State and trust

Transactional authoritative game state, with capped eight-hour offline simulation.

## First-pass limits

Asynchronous player trading and more detailed competitor/events systems remain follow-up.
